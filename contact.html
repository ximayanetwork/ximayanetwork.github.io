Questions

Frequently asked.

A few simple answers before you begin.

What is XIMAYA?

XIMAYA is an evolving ecosystem bringing together commerce, experiences, community, social connection, membership and care.

How do I join XIMAYA Social?

Visit XIMAYA Social to explore the community experience. Real accounts, persistent messaging and other advanced social functions will be connected as the platform develops.

What is XIMAYA Membership?

Membership is designed around different levels of belonging, from Family through Black, Gold, Signature and Legacy.

What is the XIMAYA Card?

The XIMAYA Card is envisioned as a unified identity connecting membership, recognition, access and the wider XIMAYA ecosystem.

Can I partner with XIMAYA?

Yes. Partnership and collaboration enquiries can be submitted through the contact form above or by email at ximayanetwork@gmail.com.
