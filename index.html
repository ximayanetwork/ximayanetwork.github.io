The Ecosystem

One brand. Many ways to connect.

XIMAYA is envisioned as a multi-purpose ecosystem where people can discover products, services, food, gifts, community opportunities and meaningful ways to contribute.

🛍️

XIMAYA Market

A marketplace for buying and selling products, supporting local commerce, sellers, discovery and business promotion.

🍽️

XIMAYA Food

A food-ordering ecosystem connecting restaurants and food businesses with customers.

🛠️

XIMAYA Services

Discover lawful service providers such as tutors, designers, mechanics, cleaners, photographers and more.

🎁

XIMAYA Gifts

Physical and digital gifting for birthdays, festivals, celebrations and special moments.

❤️

XIMAYA Care

A proposed social-impact pillar supporting verified charitable campaigns, essential needs and community initiatives.

👥

XIMAYA Family

A membership and community experience designed around recognition, belonging, selected benefits and participation.

XIMAYA Family

Membership with a sense of belonging.

XIMAYA Family is envisioned as a community experience where eligible members can receive recognition and selected benefits as the ecosystem grows.

Member Experience

Membership may include selected benefits depending on eligibility, capacity and official membership terms.

  • Member recognition
  • Selected offers and benefits
  • Early access opportunities
  • Community participation
  • Birthday and festival greetings
  • Occasional member gifts

Membership Tiers

Proposed membership tiers are designed as brand privilege concepts rather than banking or credit products.

Building in Stages

A vision that grows responsibly.

XIMAYA is intended to develop progressively rather than launch every feature simultaneously.

Stage 01

Core Marketplace

Validate demand, onboard sellers and providers, and establish the core digital experience.

Stage 02

Expand the Ecosystem

Grow into food, services, gifting and additional business tools as operational capability develops.

Stage 03

Community & Impact

Develop membership, social-impact initiatives and broader ecosystem opportunities with appropriate systems and partnerships.
